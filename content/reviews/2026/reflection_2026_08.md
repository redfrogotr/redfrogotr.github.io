Ok, let's start.

We're not always clear about our ability. As we overestimate our ability of finishing a goal, we also underestimate what we have done. I'm always thinking about what kind of people I am. Usually, my styles of doing is to build a plan at first, then I start to take an action. But sometimes I want to drift away my plan. I see myself as a taking-all-or-nothing people, and I think so most of people are. It's really hard to say which doing style is right, because we always can find some examples representing each type of people. So far, my attitude is **if we still haven't figured out what we are really obsessed in, we should trust reasonable plans**, though they are not always efficient, at least we can make sure they are effective. I see myself as normal people, and my only distinction with others is I like reflecting, like what i am doing now. by that, i can see my drawbacks continually. 

I always maintain myself in a state of that i should do something. even though watching a movie, it is in a plan. So i lack of the happiness of meeting something new, out of my expectation. Once i leave my plan, i start to prone to *instant gratification*. I cannot really focus on what i am currently doing, even anything. Coming through so a long time, a quite effective approach for me is persuading myself that i cannot do a lot. So I won't think too much. But, it stills needs practice.
